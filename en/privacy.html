Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Mote keeps your notes on your device and in your own iCloud account. Mote has no server; its developer cannot see your notes, recordings or location.

Data we collect

None. Mote uses no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and collects no information about you.

Your notes and iCloud

The notes you write and dictate, along with cards, tags, formatting and reminders, are stored on your device.

If iCloud is on, this data syncs through Apple's iCloud to your private iCloud database, so you see the same notes on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Only your Apple Account can access it, and Apple's privacy policy applies.

Microphone, speech and recordings

The microphone is used only while you hold to talk or start listening.

Speech is turned into text by Apple's on-device speech recognition; the audio does not leave your device for this.

A spoken note's recording is kept with the note on your device and, with iCloud on, in your iCloud. Deleting the note deletes its recording.

Location

With your permission, where a note was taken is saved with it (for searches like “notes I took in Izmir”). You can turn off Settings › Add location to notes in the app.

To turn coordinates into a place name, Apple Maps' service is used, which sends the location to Apple. It is sent nowhere else.

Apple Intelligence

Searching by meaning, card topics, tags and summaries use the on-device Apple Intelligence model. Your notes do not leave your device for this. Without the model, Mote works with rules.

Notifications, widgets and sharing

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Apple's silent notifications are used for iCloud sync.

Widgets and the Share extension share data only with Mote on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete any note or card, or all cards with Settings › Delete everything. Deleted cards stay in “Recently deleted” for 30 days, then are removed.

You can remove Mote's iCloud data entirely in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Deleting the app removes the data on the device.

Children

Mote collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
